**Title:** Court Rush

**Short description:** Made for the love of tennis. Free for everyone, always.

**Description:**

Made for the love of tennis.

Court Rush is a free browser tennis game for anyone who enjoys the sport. Play a
broadcast-style singles match on clay, grass or a hard court, in a packed stadium
or at a leafy country club. Take on a CPU opponent in an exhibition, or work
through a tournament draw from the quarter-finals to the final, with a two-voice
commentary booth calling the big moments.

No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no tracking. Just tennis.

**Controls:** arrows or WASD to move and aim, Space to drive (hold for power),
K to slice, L to lob, C to challenge a line call. On a phone or tablet, use the
on-screen stick and buttons.

**Thank you:** Court Rush is built on the generosity of others: motion capture
from the Bandai Namco Research Motion Dataset (CC BY-NC 4.0) and Tennis-MoCap by
Pulgarin-Giraldo et al. (CC BY-SA 3.0), bodies and clothes from MakeHuman (CC0),
and voices from Piper TTS with LibriTTS (CC BY 4.0). The game's own code is open
source under the MIT License.

**Page settings:** Kind of project: HTML. Pricing: No payments. Embed: click to
launch in fullscreen, or play in the page at 1280 x 720 with "mobile friendly"
ticked.
