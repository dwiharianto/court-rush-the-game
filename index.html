About Court Rush

Court Rush is a labour of love: a tennis game made by a fan for everyone who loves the sport.

There's nothing to buy, no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your settings and tournament progress stay on your own device.

It's built on the generosity of others: motion capture shared by researchers, open body models, and open-source voices and code. Thank you to all of them. The game's own code is open source too, so anyone can learn from it or build on it.

Enjoy the rally.

How to play

Arrows or WASDMove, and aim at contact
SpaceDrive (smash on a high ball). Hold for power, release to swing
KSlice
LLob
At the netSpace volleys, K drop volleys
ServePress once to toss, again at the top
CChallenge a line call
P or EscPause
StickMove, and aim at contact
HITDrive (smash on a high ball)
SLICE / LOBLow skidding ball, or a high one
At the netHIT volleys, SLICE drop volleys
ServeTap once to toss, again at the top

Time your swing as the ball reaches you: the timing note after each shot tells you if you were early or late. The ring on the court shows where the CPU's shot will bounce.
