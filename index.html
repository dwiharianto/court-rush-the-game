About Court Rush

Court Rush is a labour of love: a tennis game made by a fan for everyone who loves the sport.

There's nothing to buy, no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your settings and tournament progress stay on your own device.

It's built on the generosity of others: motion capture shared by researchers, open body models, and open-source voices and code. Thank you to all of them. The game's own code is open source too, so anyone can learn from it or build on it.

Enjoy the rally.
